## How ours differs (designed deliberately)

- The judge brief (in order): (1) is a weird job title, (2) stranger than
  ordinary work / gloriously odd twist, (3) funny, twist lands on first
  read, (4) sounds like a real job, specific enough to picture the person
  doing it, (5) reads instantly.
- Our title: "Professional Mourner for Strangers' Funerals" (44 chars).
  Professional mourners are a REAL job (people are hired to cry at
  funerals). The twist is one word — "Strangers'": you are paid to weep at
  the funeral of someone you never met. It is instantly picturable (a
  stranger in black, crying on cue at a funeral), lands on first read, and
  is not a generic oddity or word salad.
- It avoids every mined pattern: no corporate-parody title ("Senior Vice
  President of ..."), no "for billionaires" intensifier, no classic
  joke-job cliche, no random adjective pile.
